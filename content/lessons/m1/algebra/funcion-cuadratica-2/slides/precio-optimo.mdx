<Columnas>
<div>
Si la empresa cobra $\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x}$ miles de pesos por un tour, arrienda $40-2\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x}$ bicicletas:

$$
I(\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x})=\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x}\,(40-2\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x})=-2\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x}^2+40\htmlClass{asoc-4}{x}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|c}
x & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5\\ \hline
I(x) & 0 & 38 & 72 & 102 & 128 & 150
\end{array}
$$
